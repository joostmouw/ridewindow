| Public tester opt-in link | Anyone with the link can request to join the Google Group and, once accepted, the closed test |
| Public feedback address | Surfaced to every closed-test tester in Play |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-26-06 | Information Disclosure | Tester opt-in link and feedback address | accept | Intended — this is exactly what CON-02/03/04 ask for; low stakes for a beta test, moderated via Google Group membership |
| T-26-07 | Spoofing | Anyone with the opt-in link can request to join regardless of identity | accept | Google Group membership can still be moderated/removed after the fact; low stakes for a closed beta |
</threat_model>
