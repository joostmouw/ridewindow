Things that failed quietly now say so. Thanks for your reports.

Answering an invite, choosing a window, inviting buddies, sharing an invite
link and removing a buddy failed silently when the network failed. Each now
shows a message.

Signing out now says so when it cannot reach the server. If notifications are
switched off, turning on a reminder explains this and opens your settings.

New users can no longer get stuck on the first screen when a setting cannot
be saved.
